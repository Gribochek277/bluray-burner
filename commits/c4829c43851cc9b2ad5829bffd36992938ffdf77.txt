message:
Auto-prefer the burner that actually has a disc
With an internal DVD writer (sr0, empty) plus an external BD burner
(sr1, disc inside), the script probed only sr0 and kept reporting
'no media'. detect_device now scans /dev/sr* and switches to the first
drive that contains media; an explicitly passed --device is honored
unconditionally. media_status takes an optional device argument.
(DEVICE=/dev/sr1 is also pinned in the local .env.)
