message:
Drop case-duplicate files instead of cancelling the disc
Joliet is case-insensitive: '...Because I Don't Know!' and
'...because I DON'T KNOW!' collide on the ISO. When the colliding files
have the SAME size they are duplicates - the first one stays, the second
is dropped, marked 'skipped' in the manifest (separate from 'burned') and
the burn proceeds. Different-size collisions are real conflicts and still
abort the disc. Dry-run previews both cases per disc. Also fixed the
in-memory burned set not being updated after mark_skipped.
