Fault-tolerant progress: local shadow state + best-effort NAS sync
The manifest on the NAS is unreachable whenever the share is down, and a
power loss right after a successful burn used to lose that disc's progress
(a re-run would re-burn it). Now the project state is kept in two places:
a local shadow (~/.burn_state/<hash>.json, authoritative, always writable)
and the NAS manifest (portable replica, synced best-effort). Every change
is written to the shadow first, then to the NAS; planning reads the union,
so an outage can never lose a disc of progress or cause a re-burn.
Marks are batched per disc (one python call). Staging/caching failures
(now usually 'NAS unreachable') exit cleanly instead of looping; a real
Joliet collision also exits instead of re-planning forever.

Also restored the real project state by hand: disc #1 (67 units,
100-ANG) is now correctly marked burned in the NAS manifest, and the
run continues from 'Angry Birds Star Wars (USA)'.
