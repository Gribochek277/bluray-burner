Plan Joliet names instead of cancelling: shorten, don't fail the disc
genisoimage with -joliet-long hard-truncates every Joliet name to 103
characters, extension included, and aborts the whole build when two names
share that prefix ("have the same Joliet name", rc=255) - so a single
over-long name or a pair of long twins could kill a disc *after* 20+ GB
were staged. The old pre-check only caught the case-collision half of
this and exited on any real conflict.

Replace validate_iso_names with plan_iso_names, which plans the on-disc
names instead of giving up:

  * byte-identical copies are dropped as before (now byte-compared, not
    size-compared) and marked skipped in the project;
  * real collisions and names longer than 103 chars are shortened to fit,
    keeping the extension and the informative tail, with a stable ~N tag
    (the file's position in its sorted collision group) inside the first
    103 chars so the name stays unique after truncation.

Renames happen on the disc only: they are passed to growisofs as
-graft-points pairs, so the source files, the staging copy and the
project state keep their original names, and Rock Ridge still exposes the
full name on Linux. --dry-run prints every planned rename.

README documents the Joliet limit, the shortening rules and the
graft-points mechanism.
