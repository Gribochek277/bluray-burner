Fix false verification failure after successful BD-R burn
On BD-R SRM+POW a successful growisofs burn legitimately leaves the
track 'partial incremental' (track is closed, session close is skipped
by design), and growisofs ejects/reloads the tray afterwards - over USB
the reload can fail, so the drive reports 'no media' for a while. The
old check (track_state immediately + 'partial' == failure) declared the
burn FAILED and the disc wasted. Verified on the USA-VAN disc: burn was
100% successful (87/87 files, md5 match vs staging).

Verification is now: growisofs rc + error markers in the log; after a
clean burn, wait for the drive to present the disc (retries, then tray
close), and if it never appears, ask the user to confirm instead of
declaring the disc wasted.
